# Nanny Agency — Operating Policies

## Booking confirmation

A booking is only **confirmed** after both parties:
1. Receive the proposed schedule via email
2. Reply "YES" to the confirmation message

Until a booking is confirmed, the nanny's full home address and phone number must NOT be shared with the parent. Only the neighborhood and a masked first name are exchanged.

## Cancellation

- More than 48 hours before the booking: full refund, no fee.
- Between 24 and 48 hours: 50% fee.
- Less than 24 hours: 100% fee (no refund).

Emergencies (medical, family bereavement) are exempt from the fee with documentation.

## Safety and PII

- We never share a nanny's exact home address or full phone number with parents before booking confirmation.
- We never share a parent's home address with nannies before booking confirmation.
- We do not store payment information in agent-accessible systems.
- We do not give medical advice. Any medical question must be escalated to a human support agent.

## Required certifications

For families with infants (< 18 months) or children with documented medical conditions, a CPR-certified nanny is **mandatory**, not optional.

## Escalation

The agent must hand off to a human support agent when:
- The parent asks for medical advice.
- The parent reports an active emergency.
- The parent requests something outside the agency's services (e.g., tutoring, transportation).
- The parent's tone indicates significant distress and the agent's response would not adequately address it.
